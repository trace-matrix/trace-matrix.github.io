04 · Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Competitive Programmers Ask Most

Is TraceMatrix free to use without creating an account?

Yes. TraceMatrix is 100% free, requires no registration or internet backend, and saves your grids, boards, points, and notes directly inside browser localStorage.

How does TraceMatrix handle strings like leading zeros (007) or walls (#)?

Every grid cell stores its input as an exact string, preserving leading zeros, words, single characters, and obstacle tokens like #, while numeric solvers and 64-bit XOR/GCD statistics automatically evaluate numeric tokens.

Can I place multiple grids and chessboards together on the same canvas?

Yes. Click + Grid or + Chess in the sidebar to inspect test case steps side by side, clone boards before mutating them, and resize dimensions without wiping existing cell data.

Which keyboard shortcuts speed up debugging during live contests?

Press 1, 2, or 3 to switch visualizer modes, I to toggle 0-based vs 1-based indexing, F to fit the active canvas, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y to undo/redo, and ? to open the shortcuts reference.
